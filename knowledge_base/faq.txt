Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are you open on Sunday?
A: No. Glow & Grace Salon is closed on Sunday.

Q: Can I choose my stylist?
A: Yes. Customers may request a preferred stylist, subject to availability.

Q: Do you accept walk-ins?
A: Walk-ins may be accepted depending on availability, but appointments are recommended.

Q: Can I cancel my appointment?
A: Yes. Customers can request cancellation before the scheduled appointment time.

Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?
A: Yes. Appointments can be rescheduled depending on available time slots.

Q: Do you provide home service?
A: No. Home service is not currently available.

Q: How can I book an appointment?
A: Customers can book directly through the WhatsApp AI assistant.
